# Media rights and attribution

English · [简体中文](#简体中文)

Videos, prompts, posts, music, brands and depicted assets belong to their rights holders. Listing them here does not transfer ownership and does not make them MIT-licensed or free to reuse commercially. The MIT license covers this repository's scripts only.

Each card credits the creator and links to the original X post. Posters and videos are served by SandBase Lab, which mirrors them so they play where X is unavailable. Model attribution follows the creator's own statement.

To correct a credit or remove a video, [open an issue](https://github.com/sandbase-lab/awesome-opus-5.5/issues/new) with the post URL. We remove it from SandBase Lab, including the mirrored files, and it disappears from this list on the next daily sync.

Not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic.
